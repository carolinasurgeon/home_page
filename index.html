Professional Summary

Fellow American College of Surgeons, board certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon with dual MD and DDS degrees with three decades of clinical, academic and leadership experience. Former Assistant Professor of Surgery and examiner for the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. Published researcher with seminal contributions to craniofacial genetics, including co-identification of Oculo-Facio-Cardio-Dental (Gorlin-Marashi) Syndrome. Extensive experience in private practice management, surgery, anesthesia, trauma, and multidisciplinary care.
